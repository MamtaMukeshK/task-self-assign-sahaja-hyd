# Telugu and Hindi demo videos: draft wording for a native speaker's check

Status: approved by the user and recorded on 2026-10-01 (docs/demo-te.mp4, docs/demo-hi.mp4). NOT yet checked by a
native speaker. To change a line: spoken words are in te/lines.json and hi/lines.json, captions and cards in the
`T` table of ../record_demo.js; then re-record (HANDOFF.md, "Re-record the demo").

How to check: read each Telugu or Hindi line aloud. Is it natural, polite, and easy for an elderly volunteer?
Mark any word you would say differently. The English "back-translation" under each line says what it literally
means, so a reviewer who reads only one of the two languages can still check meaning.

Rules used:
- Button and section names are copied exactly from what the page shows in that language, so the viewer can
  find them on screen. The "Register" button reads నమోదు చేయండి / पंजीकरण करें (renamed on 2026-10-01 from
  ఎంచుకోండి / चुनें, "Choose", so the page and the narration use one word for registering).
- Spoken lines have no quote marks and write numbers as words (the voice engines read them better that way).
  Captions on screen use quote marks around button names, like the English video.
- Last step: the English video says "Prefer Telugu or Hindi?"; the Telugu video says "Want English or Hindi?"
  and the Hindi video says "Want English or Telugu?".
